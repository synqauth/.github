# Synq

Customer identity for the apps you ship.

Synq is an OpenID Connect issuer for your business. Every brand gets its own issuer, its own users and its own sign-in page, with Solana wallet, social and email sign-in side by side, a management API, webhooks and an audit log.

- Site: [synq.build](https://synq.build)
- Docs: [docs.synq.build](https://docs.synq.build)
- SDKs: the [`@synqauth`](https://www.npmjs.com/org/synqauth) packages on npm
- X: [@synqauth](https://x.com/synqauth)

## Repositories

There are no public repositories here yet. The SDKs are built in Synq's private repository, and their guides and reference are at [docs.synq.build](https://docs.synq.build).
